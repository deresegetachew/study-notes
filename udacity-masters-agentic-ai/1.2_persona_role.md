# Topic 1.2: Persona / Role

> Module: Agentic AI · Date: 2026-08-27

**Q: What does a Persona (Role) do in a prompt?**
Defines how the agent should behave — its personality, tone, and perspective.

**Q: Why is assigning a role so effective?**
LLMs are trained on broad, diverse data, so without guidance they can default to any tone or style — formal, enthusiastic, casual, humorous. A role narrows this down, directing the response's style and focus to match the task at hand.

**Remember:** no role → unpredictable style, less targeted output. Defined role → controlled tone and more precise, on-target results.
